Hi! I’m Sanoj. This is the plain-text version of a page drawn entirely with sine waves.

I’m an AI engineer. I like to clean, play with and transform large, confusing data into beautiful search systems with visuals.

Give me a messy pile of data and I’ll make it behave. EpigCorpus started as a heap of Latin inscriptions. Now you can search it and see it in visuals, so you can look things up in stone-carved Latin. FinAgent is a Telegram bot that lives on a Raspberry Pi 5, runs Llama 3.2 3B, and answers “how much did I spend?” from your transactions, no spreadsheet digging needed. In a past life, I turned big tables into Power BI and Tableau dashboards.

Mac apps and UI/UX are my playground. With AI-assisted coding and zero Swift experience, I built KlipKlik, a clipboard manager that reads text from images, and MacMail, a native Gmail client, plus a few small tools for everyday work and fun.

Away from the keyboard, I draw pencil portraits. Yes, they’re beautiful. You can check one out here.
